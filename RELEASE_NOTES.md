## Elite Organizer v1.0

First public release.

### What's inside
- Students with session counter, schedule days/time and notes
- Mark Paid with an editable amount; payments log with date and time
- Weekly schedule view
- Goals with percentage progress, quick amounts and priority badges; Purchases history
- USD, EUR, GBP, SYP and LBP with editable exchange rates
- Arabic and English, light and dark mode, animated splash screen
- 100% offline, no internet permission

### Install
1. Download `EliteOrganizer-v1.0.apk` below.
2. Open it on your phone and allow installing from this source if Android asks.

**Requires Android 6.0+.** On Android 6/7 keep *Android System WebView* updated from Google Play.
