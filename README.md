## Download

Get the latest APK from the **[Releases page](../../releases/latest)**: download `EliteOrganizer-vX.Y.apk` and install it on your phone.

> Android will ask you to allow installing from an unknown source the first time (Settings → Security / Apps → allow your browser or file manager to install apps). This is normal for any APK that is not installed from Google Play.

**Requirements:** Android 6.0 (API 23) or newer. On Android 6 and 7, keep *Android System WebView* updated from Google Play.

## Features

- **Students** – add a student with price per session, currency, session days and time, and notes. A session counter shows how much is due. Tap **Mark Paid**, enter the amount you actually received (you decide the amount) and the counter resets to zero.
- **Schedule** – a weekly view of all sessions sorted by time, with today highlighted.
- **Payments log** – every payment is saved with its exact date and time. Deleting a payment adjusts the student's total.
- **Goals** – track what you want to buy (for example a new phone) with a percentage progress ring, quick-add amounts, and priority badges (🥇 🥈 🥉). When the price is reached, tap **Mark Purchased**.
- **Purchases** – the history of everything you bought, with the option to move an item back to goals.
- **5 currencies** – USD, EUR, GBP, Syrian Pound (SYP) and Lebanese Pound (LBP). Every student and goal keeps its own currency, and totals can be shown in any currency.
- **Exchange rates** – editable rates against the US dollar plus a built-in snapshot of recent rates. *Update exchange rates* applies the built-in snapshot (the app never goes online).
- **Arabic and English** – full right-to-left support, switchable in Settings.
- **Light and dark mode**, a glass-style interface inspired by Apple design, smooth animations and an animated splash screen.
- **Settings** – theme, language, exchange rates, app information and FAQ.

## Privacy

Elite Organizer has **no internet permission**. All data (students, payments, goals, purchases, settings) is stored locally on your device and is never sent anywhere. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it deletes everything.
